# Class Definitions

## Normal Communication

Regular conversation that does not contain manipulation, coercion, emotional pressure, or psychological influence.

Examples:

* How was your day?
* Let's meet tomorrow.

---

## Gaslighting

A manipulation technique that causes a person to doubt their memory, perception, or understanding of reality.

Indicators:

* Denying events that occurred
* Questioning someone's memory
* Making someone feel confused about reality

Examples:

* That never happened.
* You're imagining things again.

---

## Guilt Tripping

A manipulation technique that uses guilt to influence another person's actions or decisions.

Indicators:

* Mentioning sacrifices
* Highlighting past favors
* Making someone feel responsible for emotional disappointment

Examples:

* After everything I've done for you.
* I sacrificed so much for you.

---

## Emotional Blackmail

A manipulation technique that uses fear, obligation, threats, or consequences to force compliance.

Indicators:

* Conditional affection
* Threats of abandonment
* Emotional pressure

Examples:

* If you loved me, you'd do this.
* If you leave me, I'll never forgive you.

---

## Love Bombing

A manipulation technique involving excessive affection, admiration, and idealization to create emotional dependency.

Indicators:

* Excessive compliments
* Unrealistic attachment
* Intense affection in a short period

Examples:

* You're perfect.
* You're my soulmate.
